GTI Utilities - Changelog

2026-06-14
- Added the "animal augmentation bench" (GTI_AnimalBionicsTable): an animal-themed workbench
  dedicated to A Dog Said... animal prosthetics.
    - Veterinary-themed artwork derived from EPOE's TableBionics (recoloured blue->green with a
      glowing paw decal, baked into standalone PNGs); self-contained at runtime, personal use
      only. Built from Steel + industrial components; unlocked by the animal "Animal prosthetics"
      research (SimpleAnimalProsthetics).
    - All A Dog Said craft recipes (simple prosthetics, surrogate organs, bionics, synthetic
      organs) now craft EXCLUSIVELY at this bench and no longer appear on the vanilla machining
      table / fabrication bench or EPOE's TableBionics / TableOrgans.
    - Pure XML, no assemblies. Guarded by mod presence; load GTI Utilities after A Dog Said and
      EPOE-Forked (declared via loadAfter).

2026-06-09

    Recon armor    +10%

    Marine armor   +20%

  This stacks with other gear that provides toxic environment resistance.
