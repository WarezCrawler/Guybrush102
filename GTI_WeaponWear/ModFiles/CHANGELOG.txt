GTI Weapon Wear - Changelog
RimWorld 1.6. Newest first.


2026-07-04
- Urgent auto-repair for badly worn weapons. Pawns who are busy around the clock never reach the spare-time repair, so their weapon could keep degrading. When an equipped weapon falls below a QUARTER of the configured auto-repair threshold (e.g. threshold 50% -> below 12.5% condition), the repair now becomes urgent and jumps ahead of regular work, meals and recreation - though it still yields to emergencies (firefighting, urgent work, actual starvation). Bench repair bills are unaffected.
- Auto-repair no longer preempts regular work. The equipped-weapon auto-repair was inserted into the think tree next to the apparel optimizer, which (it turns out) sits BEFORE the main work giver - so a pawn with a worn weapon would go repair it ahead of all normal work. It now uses RimWorld's official Humanlike_PostMain insertion hook, which runs after needs, joy and work: pawns only repair their weapon in genuine spare time, as always intended (and documented).
- Fixed auto-repair silently stalling after loading another save or starting a new colony in the same session. Internal per-pawn timers stored absolute game-time stamps and were never reset, so a freshly loaded game could inherit far-future timestamps from the previous one and pawns would not auto-repair (and material warnings would not fire) for a long time. All such state is now cleared on every game start/load.
- Repairs no longer consume unrelated items lying on the bench. A repair used to charge everything staged in the bench's ingredient cells - including leftover ingredients from an interrupted crafting bill or a previous repair. It now charges exactly the item's computed repair cost; anything else on the bench is left alone for the haulers.
- An interrupted repair no longer double-charges when it resumes. The re-issued job recomputes the cost from the remaining damage only, and any materials still staged from the first attempt count toward it.
- Fixed repair of items with no material cost (no recipe cost and not made of a material, e.g. a thrumbo horn). The auto-repair job for such a weapon had nothing to haul and failed with an error every time it was tried; it now goes straight to the bench and repairs for free, matching the zero cost.
- Repairs now survive saving and loading mid-job cleanly. Previously a load during a repair cancelled the job (and, in a rare case with wood staged as material, could confuse the mod about WHICH item was being repaired, because a wood log technically counts as a weapon). The repaired item is now remembered across the save and the repair resumes where it left off, charging only what is still owed.
- Internal: the two repair jobs (bench bill and equipped weapon) now share one code path, and the bench search uses the same danger tolerance as the material search.
- Fixed a repair bill flooding the log with "started 10 jobs in one tick" errors. Items stored inside a container building (such as an outfit stand / armor rack) are held away from the map and can't be carried out by the repair job, but the work giver was still offering them, so the job failed and was re-issued every tick. Such items are now skipped (a damaged item must be a free, reachable item to be auto-repaired - take it out of the stand to repair it). Repair of loose / stockpiled / shelved gear is unchanged.
- Fixed a repair bill stalling the workbench. When a repair bill was placed above other bills and its materials were missing, the bench would go idle instead of moving on to the bills below it. A repair bill that can't be funded now steps aside so the bench proceeds to the next bill in the list as expected.
- Performance: a repair bill whose materials are missing now rests for a short while (the same ~8 in-game-minute cooldown vanilla applies to bills that are out of ingredients) instead of being fully re-evaluated by every crafting pawn on every work scan. Once materials arrive, repairs resume within that cooldown, exactly like a vanilla bill. Player-forced work ignores the cooldown.
- Performance: the material search now checks the map's total resource count first, so discovering that the colony is simply out of a material costs (almost) nothing instead of examining every stack on the map.
- Performance: the "Repair needs: ..." inspect line on a damaged weapon or apparel is now cached briefly instead of being recomputed every frame while the item is selected.


2026-06-08
- Debug logging toggle. A new "Debugging" section in the mod options adds an "Enable debug logging" checkbox (off by default). When on, the mod writes diagnostic "[GTI Weapon Wear]" lines to the dev log / Player.log: equipped-weapon auto-repair decisions and why they fail, when a repair job is issued, when the material search begins, and a one-line summary when each repair finishes (item, HP before -> after, materials consumed). Lines on busy code paths are rate-limited per pawn so they can't flood the log; leave the toggle off for normal play.
- Settings window now scrolls, so longer option lists (including the new Debugging section) can no longer be clipped off the bottom of the window.
- Equipped-weapon auto-repair now re-checks at most once per 3 in-game hours per pawn (previously far more often). A worn weapon is treated as routine maintenance rather than an emergency, which also cuts background work and log noise. Manual right-click "Repair <weapon> now" is still immediate.


2026-06-07
- Repair bills now exclude tainted and biocoded gear by default. New repair bills start with "allow tainted apparel" and "allow biocoded weapons/apparel" turned off; tick them back on per bill if you want to repair such items. (Equipped-weapon auto-repair is unaffected and still repairs biocoded weapons.)
- Repair Workbench compatibility. When Acruid's "Repair Workbench" mod is active, all GTI repairs are rerouted to its dedicated repair bench (ElectricTableRepair) instead of the vanilla machining / smithy / tailoring / fabrication benches, serviced by a Crafting work giver. The bench shows two repair bills: "repair weapon" (weapons) and "repair apparel" (all apparel in one bill).
- Repair routing is now data-driven. Where (and if) an item is repaired comes from a per-item RepairProperties modExtension (a list of benches; empty = never repairable). Any mod can route or block its own gear with a PatchOperationAddModExtension - no C# needed - and equipped-weapon auto-repair follows the same data (no longer hardwired to the machining table). Items without a tag keep the built-in fallback (weapons -> machining, apparel by craft bench / material), which can itself be switched off so that only explicitly tagged items are repairable. The three apparel filters were generalised to four per-recipe filters.
- Auto-repair on/off switch. A new checkbox is the master control for equipped-weapon auto-repair; the HP-threshold slider (hidden when off) only sets the trigger level.
- Options menu reorganised into a logical progression: Weapon wear -> Repairs -> Automatic repair.


2026-06-06
- Manual repair override. Select a pawn and right-click a repair bench -> "Repair <weapon> now" forces an immediate repair to full, ignoring the auto-repair threshold (greyed out with the shortfall when material is missing).
- Out-of-material heads-up. A light top-left message fires when a pawn wants to auto-repair its own weapon but the colony lacks the material (throttled to about once per pawn per day; personal repairs only).
- Equipped-weapon auto-repair no longer needs a work type. Moved to a think-tree job giver (next to apparel optimization), so combat pawns with crafting disabled still auto-repair their weapon. Runs in spare time only.
- Repair material feedback. Damaged weapons/apparel show a "Repair needs: Nx Material (have M)" line on their inspect panel; bench bills also report a "not enough materials" reason on right-click.
- Equipped-weapon auto-repair. Undrafted pawns carry materials to a bench and repair their own equipped weapon (kept equipped) when it falls below a configurable HP threshold (default 50%).
- Weapon wear on use (the core mechanic). Each shot or melee swing has a chance to lose 1 HP: 0.10 x tear multiplier x quality multiplier. Two settings sliders (tear multiplier, quality influence). Worn weapons stop being used at 1 HP, so use alone never destroys them; 0 HP (from other damage) means destroyed and unrepairable.
- Utility apparel repair. Belt-slot gear (shields, packs, etc.) repairs at the fabrication bench. Work givers consolidated from four to two (one Smithing, one Tailoring).
- Apparel repair. Armor repairs at the smithy, clothing at the tailoring bench, routed by an apparel classifier; bill lists show only the matching category.
- Weapon repair (bench). Incremental in-place repair at the machining table: pay-before material consumption, cost computed from the weapon's own original materials (components excluded), scaled by damage, at a configurable fraction (default 25%). Quality and material are preserved.
- Initial scaffolding. Pipeline proof (XML patch + C# + Harmony) tagging weapons in the inspect panel; superseded and removed once real features landed.
